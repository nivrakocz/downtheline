Support

Down the Line is a surfing game for iPhone: ride a long right-hand wave, surf heats against other surfers, free surf, or learn the moves in short lessons.

Contact

Questions, problems or ideas? Email us at nivrakocz@gmail.com and we will get back to you.

Please tell us your iPhone model and what happened, and attach a screenshot if you can.

How to play

  • Tap the right side of the screen to turn up the wave, the left side to turn down it.
  • Turn up and down the face to make speed and stay ahead of the whitewater.
  • Double tap and hold for the big moves - snaps, off the lips and roundhouses. Learn to Play in the menu teaches each one, with a demo.
