Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Down the Line ("the game") is made by Niv Rakocz. This policy explains what the game collects and why. In short: nothing personal, and anonymous play data only if you agree.

What we do not collect

The game does not ask for or collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location or any account details. There are no ads, and the game does not track you across other apps or websites.

Anonymous play data - only with your consent

The first time you open the game it asks whether you want to share anonymous play data. If you choose No thanks, nothing is sent. If you choose Yes, the game sends, through Unity Analytics:

  • How you play: which mode you choose, which surf spot, how each wave ends, its score and length, lessons passed and demos watched, and heat results.
  • The subscription offer: when it is shown, whether a free trial was offered, and whether a purchase or a restore went through. Never any payment details.
  • Technical information: a random ID created for the game on your device (not linked to you), device model, operating system and game version, and the country, worked out from your connection.

We use this only to understand how the game is played and to improve it. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising.

Unity Technologies processes this data for us. See Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Subscription and purchases

The game offers an optional subscription that opens more surf spots. It is bought, renewed and cancelled through Apple's App Store: Apple handles the payment, and we never see your card, your Apple ID or any payment details. The game only asks the App Store whether you have an active subscription and until when, and keeps that on your device so the spots stay open without a connection. You can manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple ID settings.

Crash reports

If the game crashes, a crash report is sent through Unity Diagnostics so we can fix it: the device model, operating system, game version and technical details of the crash. It contains nothing personal and is not linked to you. Reports of smaller errors in the game's code are sent only if you agreed to share play data.

Data stored on your device

Your progress, best scores, settings and whether you have the subscription are saved on your iPhone only. Deleting the game deletes them (the subscription itself stays with your Apple ID and can be restored).

Your choices

You can stop sharing play data at any time with Share play data in the game's Settings, or by deleting the game, and you can ask us to delete play data linked to your device's random ID by emailing us.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.
